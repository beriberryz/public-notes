>>## Terms
>>**1. Area** - A major section of the bibliographic description, comprising data or set of categories
>>**2. Element** - a word or group of characters presenting a distinct unit of bibliographic information and forming a part of an area of the desc
>>
>>## Areas of Descriptions
>>
>>### 1. **Title and statement of responsibility**
>>#### A. **Title Proper**
>>- The chief name of an item, including any alternative title but excluding parallel titles and other title information
>>- Transcribe the title proper exactly as to wording, order, and spelling, but not necessarily as to punctuation and capitalization. Capitalize the first word then the rest of the words capitalize only when its a proper noun
>>- Abridge a long title proper only if this can be done without loss of essential information
>>- Supple a title proper for an item lacking a chief source of information from the rest of the item, or a reference source, or elsewhere
>>- [[01 - Notes/General Rules for Description and Describing Monographs using AACR2#^GMD\|General Material Designation (GMD)]]
>>##### ** Parallel Title**
>>- The title proper in another language and/or script
>>- Precede by an equals sign
>>- `Twilight = Takipsilm`
>>##### ** Other Title Information**
>>- A title borne by an item other than the title proper or parallel or series title(s); also any phrase appearing in conjunction with the title proper, etc., indicative of the character, contents, etc., of the item or the motives for, or occasion of, its production or publication
>>##### **Subtitles**
>>- Precede by a colon
>>- `Principle of economics : an introduction`
>>### **B. Statement of Responsibility**
>>- Which entity has the contribution in creating the bibliographic record
>>-  Only put those who are in the title page
>>- Exclude titles or suffixes
>>- Precede by a diagonal slash
>>	- `Introduction to bilogy / Fred Williams`
>>- If a single statement of responsibility names more than three persons or corporate bodies performing the same function, or with the same degree of responsibility, omit all but the first of each group of such persons or bodies. Indicate the omission by the mark of omission and add et al. in square brackets
>>	- `Cataloging in libraries / Renato Gomez ... [et al.]`
>>- More than one statement of responsibility, transcribe in the order indicated by their sequence on, or the layout of, the chief source of information
>>- Precede each subsequent statement of responsibility by a semicolon
>>	- `Introductory chemistry / by William Ford ... [et al.] ; edited by James Richards ; with a foreword by Terry Fletcher`
>>- Add a word or short phrase to the statement of responsibility if the relationship between the title of the item and the person(s) or body (bodies) named in the statement of responsibility is not clear, Enclose it in square brackets
>>	- `Mga piling tula ng pag-ibig / [collected by] Brando Tatlonghari`
>>
>>### 2. Edition
>>- **Edition Statement**
>>	- As found on the item, use the abbreviation
>>	- `3rd ed.` Source of information reads: Third Edition
>>	- `Rev. 4th ed.` Source of information reads: Revised 4th Edition
>>	- `5^e [ed.]` Source of information reads: 5^e
>>- **Statement of Responsibility Relating to the Edition**
>>	- Transcribe a statement of responsibility relating to one or more editions, but not all editions of a given work following the edition statement if there is one
>>	- Preceded by a diagonal slash
>> - `Classification in libraries / written by Efren Tumulak. -- 4th ed. / edited by Ricardo Dimatulac.`
>>### 3. Material specific details
>>   - Used in the description of:
>> 	  - Cartographic Materials
>> 		  - Scale ; statement of projection
>> 	- Music
>> 		- Musical presentation statement (e.g., playing score)
>> 	- Continuing resources (called Numbering Area)
>> 		- Numeric and/or alphabetic designation (e.g., Vol 1, no 1-v, no. 2)
>> 	- Microforms
>> 		- called Special Data for Cartographic Materials, Music and Serials Area; applicable only if the content of the microform is/are cartographic materials, music, and/or continuing resources
>>### 4. Publication, distribution, etc. (Imprint)
>>- **Place of publication, distribution, etc.**
>>	- You don't need to put the entire address, city is enough, add state if it is stated. No need to indicate the county unless required
>>	- If two or more places has offices are named in the item, indicate the first named place
>>	- Indicate any subsequently named place that is given prominence by layout or typography in the source of info of the resource. Precede each subsequently named place by a semicolon
>>		- `Manila ; Tokyo` Source of information reads: Manila . London . TOKYO . New Delhi . Seoul
>>	- If the first named place an any place given prominence are not in the home country of the cataloguing agency, give also the first of any subsequently named place that is in the home country. Then omit all other place
>>		- `New York ; London ; Sydney` Source of information reads: New York . LONDON . Sydney; cataloging agency is located in Australia
>>- **Uncertain Place**
>>	- Probable place is known: Give it in brackets → `[Manila]`
>>	- Place is uncertain: Add `?` → `[Quezon City?]`
>>	- Only country/state/province is known: Give it in brackets → `[Philippines]`
>>	- Country/state/province is uncertain: Add `?` → `[Canada?]`
>>	- No place can be determined: Use `[S.I.]` (_sine loco_ = “without a place”)
>>	- Unpublished item: **Do not record a place** and **do not use `[S.I.]`**.
>>- **Name of publisher, distribution, etc.**
>>	- Give the name following the place(s) to which it relates
>>	- Give the name in the shorter form in which it can be understood and identified internationally
>>	- Precede by a color
>>	- `ABS-CBN Pub.` Source of information reads: ABS-CBN Publishing, Inc.
>>	- If unknown, give : [s.n.] = sone nomine
>>	- Do not record if unpublished, do not also indicate s.n.
>>- **Date of publication, distribution, etc.**
>>	- Precede by a comma
>>	- Give the date as indicated in the resource, even if it is incorrect. Add the correct date `, 2107 [i.e. 2017]`
>>	- Replace the copyright and phonogram copyright symbol with c and p ` , 2017, c2016`
>>	- If no date, supply an approximate date of publication
>> 	- `, [2014 or 2015]`
>> 	- `, [2015?]`
>> 	- `, [ca. 1980]`
>> 	- ` , [198-]`
>> 	- ` , [198-?]`
>>- **Place of manufacture, name of manufacturer, date of manufacture**
>> - Give the place **only if** the name of publisher is unknown; and the place and name of the manufacturer are found in the resource
>> - If the date is already indicated in place of an unknown date of pub, distribution, etc., do not repeat it
>> - Enclose the place of manufacture, name of manufacture, and date of manufacture in parentheses
>> 	- `[S.l. : s.n.], 2014 printing (Manila : Philippine Experts)`
>>### 5. Physical Description
>>- **Number of volumes and/or pagination**
>>	- ***Single volumes***
>>		- `1033 p.`, `345 leaves`, `ix, 657 p.`, `56 p., 200 leaves`
>>		- If unnumbered and the number of pages or leaves is readily ascertainable, give the number in square brackets. Bibilangin bawat isa
>>		- If the number is not readily ascertainable, estimate the number of pages or leaves and give that estimated number without square brackets and preceded by ca.
>>			- `[104] p.`, `ca. 400 p.`
>>		- If the volume has complicated or **irregular paging**, give the pagination using one of the following methods
>>			1. Give the total number of pages or leaves followed by "in various pagings" or "in various foliations"
>>				- `1000 p. in various pagings`
>>			2. Give the number of pages or leaves in the main sequences of the pagination and add the total number, in square brackets, of the remaining various paged sequences
>>				- `234, [53] p.`
>>			1. Give `1 v. (various pagings)`
>>		- Plate - "a leaf containing illustrative matter, with or without explanatory text, that does not form part of either the preliminary or the main sequence of pages or leaves"
>>			- `246 p., 32 p. of plates`
>>	- ***Publications in more than one volume***
>>		- Give the number of volumes of a printed monograph in more than one physical volume, `3 v.`
>>		- If a set of volumes is continuously paged, indicate the pagination in parentheses after the number of volumes. Ignore separately paged sequences of preliminary content in volumes other than the first, `4 v. (xx, 934 p.)`
>
>>[!blank]
>>- **Illustrative matter**
>>	- Precede by a colon
>>	- Give `ill.` for an illustrated monograph
>>		- if the publications consists wholly or predominantly of illustrations, give `all ill.` or `chiefly ill.`
>>	- `col.` if colored
>>		- if all illustrations are colored `col. ill.`
>>		- if majority of illustrations colored, use `ill. (chiefly col.)`
>>		- if majority are not colored, use `ill. (some col.)`
>>		- if monochromic, only use `ill.`
>>- **Dimensions**
>>	- Precede by a semicolon
>>	- Give the height of the item in cm, to the next whole cm up
>>	- If the item measures less than 10 cm, give the height in mm
>>	- If the width of the volume is either less than half of the height or greater than the height, give the height x width
>>	- If the volumes in a multipart resource differ in size, indicate the smallest or smaller and the largest or the larger size, separated by a hyphen `; 20-32 cm`
>>- **Accompanying material**
>>	- Material issued with, and intended to be used with, the item being catalogued
>>	- Four ways in describing accompanying materials:
>>	  1. Create a separate record
>>	  2. Create a multilevel description
>>	     - Catalogue the main book then the accompanying material
>>	  3. Indicate in a note
>>	  4. Record in the Physical Description area
>>	     - Indicate the number of physical units in Arabic numerals and the name of the accompanying material
>>	     - Precede it by a plus sign
>>	     - `271 p. : ill. ; 21 cm. + 1 answer book`
>>	- [[01 - Notes/General Rules for Description and Describing Monographs using AACR2#^SMD\|Specific Material Designation]]
>>### 6. Series
>>- Enclose each series in parentheses
>>- Elements:
>>	- Title proper of series
>>	  - If a resource is issued in a series, transcribe the title proper of the series
>>	- Parallel title of series
>>	  - Transcribe any parallel titles of a series following the rules for parallel titles
>>	- Other title information of series
>>	  - Indicate the other title information of series only if necessary to identify the series
>>	  - Transcribe it following the rules for other titles information
>>	  - `World history : a collection of essays`
>>	- Statements of responsibility relating to series
>>	  - Transcribe statements of responsibility relating to the series title only if considered to be necessary for identification of the series
>>	  - Precede it by a diagonal slash
>>	  - `Research monographs / Institute of Economic Affairs`
>>	- ISSN of series
>>	  - Give the ISSN of a series if it appears in the item being described
>>	  - Precede by a comma
>>	  - `(Western Canada series report, ISSN 0317-3127`
>>	- Numbering within series
>>	  - Indicate the numbering of the item within the series in the terms given in the resource
>>	  - Use abbreviations and numerals
>>	  - Precede by a semicolon
>>	  - `; no. 3`
>>### 7. Note
>>- Useful descriptive information, which cannot be presented in the other areas, is given in notes
>>- Precede each note by a full stop, space, dash, space, or start a new paragraph `. -- `
>>- Types and Order of Notes in the Area:
>>  1. Nature, scope, or artistic form (including frequency of continuing resources and system requirements and mode of access for electronic resources)
>>     - Quarterly
>>     - Mode of access: World Wide Web
>>     - System requirements: Windows 10
>>     - Documentary
>>	2. Language of the item and/or translation or adaptation if it is not apparent in the title
>>	   - Translation of: El filibusterismo
>>	   - Commentary in Filipino
>>	3. Source of title proper (from cover, from table of contents page)
>>	4. Variations in title (indicate any title by which a resource is commonly known or any title in the resource which differs from the title proper)
>>	   - Cover title: Biochemistry crash course
>>	   - Spine title: Microbiology and parasitology
>>	   - Commonly known as: The Avengers
>>	5. Parallel titles and other title information
>>	   - Parallel title on back of container insert: Tinagong paraiso
>>	   - Subtitle on cover: Traditional dances in the Philippines
>>	6. Statements of responsibility
>>	   - "Based on the book by Bob Ong"--Cover
>>	7. Edition and history
>>	   - Rev. Ed. of: Cataloging and classification
>>	   - Previous ed.: Manila : National Book Store, 2005
>>	   - Changed title and/or publication
>>	8. Material specific details
>>	   - File size varies
>>	   - Numbering begins each year with no. 1 (continuing resources)
>>	9. Publication, distribution, etc.
>>	10. Physical description
>>	11. Accompanying material and supplements
>>	12. Series
>>	13. Dissertations (indicate if the resource is a dissertation or a thesis. Indicate "Thesis", followed by the degree for which the author was a candidate, enclosed in parentheses, the name of the institution the dissertation/thesis was presented, and the year the degree was granted.)
>>	    - `Thesis (M.L.I.S)--University of the Philippines Diliman, 2017`
>>	14. Audience
>>	15. Reference to published description (used primarily for manuscripts)
>>	16. Other formats 
>>	17. Summary (You may simply copy the summary or synopsis, if available)
>>	18. Contents
>>	    - Can be:
>>		     - Coverage of each volumes
>>		     - If there's an index or bibliography (put page if there's a bibliography)
>>		     - If the references are seen after each chapter, no need to put pagination of bibliography, just `Includes bibliographical references`, because it is dispersed
>>	19. Numbers associated with the item (other than those presented in the Standard number and terms of availability area)
>>	    - If the resource has a particular unique identifier
>>	20. Copy being described, library's holding, and restriction on use
>>	   - Can be what is unique to the copy or imperfection (`Library lacks v.4` or `Library copy signed on t.p.: Jose Rizal`)
>>	   - Can also be the restriction on which users can only use
>>	21. "With" notes (for resources which contain more than one work i.e. a book which contained Noli me Tangere and El Filibusterismo and was bound as tete-beche)
>>	   - `With: El Filibusterismo`
>>	22. Combined notes relating to the original
>>	   - Facsimile copies of resources
>>	23. Item described (for serials, integrating resources, and multipart items, if the description is not based on the first issue, part, or iteration)
>>   - `Latest ssued consulted: 1999/10`
>>   - `Description based on: Vol. 1, no. 3 (Aug 1999).`
>>### 8. Standard Number and Terms of Availability
>>- Precede this area by a fullt stop, space, dash, space or start a new paragraph
>>- Not used for manuscripts
>>- Some frequently used elements:
>>	- Standard number
>>		- ISBD/ISSN
>>		- or any international number with the standard spacing or hyphenation
>>		- Optionally, give more than one number for an item which bears two or more such numbers and add a qualification
>>	- Terms of availability
>>		- Optional
>>		- Indicate the terms on which the resource is available
>>		- Consists of the price if the resource is for sale, or a brief statement of other terms if the resource is not for sale
>>		- Precede it by a colon
>>		- `ISBN 0-7225-0344-X : $8.75`
>>		- `Access limited to subscribers`
>>	- Qualification
>>		- Indicate a brief qualification after the standard number when a resource bears two or more standard numbers
>>		- If a resource has only one standard number, add the type of binding if considered to be important
>>		- Enclose it in parentheses
>>		- `ISBN 0-684-14258-0 (pbk. : alk. paper)`

> [!info|bg-c-orange] # Specific Material Designation
>- A term indicating the special class of material (usually the class of physical object) to which an item belongs
>- Examples:
>	- For sound recordings: sound disc, sound cassette
>	- For videorecordings: video disc, video tape
>	- For microfilm: microfiche. microfilm
>- If none of the terms listed in AACR2 is appropriate, indicate the specific name of the resource as concisely as possible. As an option, use a term in common usage
>- Examples:
>	- DVD-audio
>	- DVD-video

>[!info|bg-c-orange] # Some Rules on Punctuation
>- **Put `. -` before every new area**, except the first area.  
> → Use a **period, space, dash, space** (`. -`) to separate different areas of a catalog record, unless you start the area on a new paragraph.
>- **Usually put a space before and after punctuation marks.**  
>→ For example: `Title : Subtitle`  
> → However, some punctuation marks are exceptions.
>- **Do NOT put a space before these marks:**  
> **comma (,), period (.), hyphen (-), closing parenthesis ()), and closing square bracket (])**.  
> → Correct: `Manila, Philippines`  
> → Correct: `2nd ed.`  
> → Correct: `New York)`  
> → Not: `Manila , Philippines`
> - **Do NOT put a space after these marks:**  
> **hyphen (-), opening parenthesis ((), and opening square bracket ([)**.  
>→ Correct: `1980-1985`  
> → Correct: `(second edition)`  
> → Correct: `[Place of publication]`  
> → Not: `( second edition)` or `[ Place of publication]`
> - **Put information in square brackets `[ ]` if it comes from somewhere other than the prescribed source.**  
>→ If the information you need is **not found in the required/main source**, but you get it from another source, put it in **square brackets**.  
> → Example: `[Manila]` means the cataloguer supplied the place of publication from a source outside the prescribed source.

>[!info|bg-c-orange] # Inaccuries
>|Situation|What to do|Example|
>|---|---|---|
>|**Word is misspelled**|Copy the word **exactly as it appears** in the resource.|Resource says **“criticl”** → `Principles of criticl care`|
>|**Want to point out the mistake**|Add **[sic]** after the incorrect word.|`Principles of criticl [sic] care`|
>|**Want to show the correction**|Use **i.e.** followed by the correct word in **[ ]**.|`Principles of criticl i.e. [critical] care`|
>|**Letter or letters are missing**|Supply the missing letter(s) in **[ ]**.|Resource says **“Principles of critic care”** → `Principles of [critical] care`|
