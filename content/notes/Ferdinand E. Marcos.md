# Early Ferdinand E. Marcos
- son of Mariano Marcos who was a Congressman in Ilocos Norte
- took the bar exam in 1939 and was a topnatcher
- Ang thesis niya ay tungkol sa authoritarianism

## Nalundasan Case
- Mariano lost his campaign for re-election to the House of Representatives (Congressman in Ilocos Norte) to Julio Nalundasan
- On the day after the election, Nalundasan was killed by a single rifle shot at his home in Batas
- Two witnesses accused Ferdinand Marcos who was still studying law anda national champion as a member of the university rifle team
- Ferdinand was sentenced to 10 to 17 years in prison
- While in prison, he studied for the bar and topnotched
- He appealed his conviction to the Supreme Court of the Philippines where he represented himself
- The Judge was [[Pagsasarili at Pambansang Pagpapasya#Laurel at Osemeña|Jose P. Laurel]] and wrote the majority decision, sabi niya "sayang naman ang talent ni Marcos kung ikukulong lang"

## World War II
- Formed the guerilla called Maharlika
- Marami siyang award pero parang peke lang
- Yung mga medal niya is nag cause na sumikat siya sa masa or nag boost para sa posisyon niya sa politiko

## Political Rise
- Entered the politics as a member of the new Liberal Party
- 1949: ran for his father's former seat in Ilocos Norte's 2nd congressional district; He won and was re-elected to the HR twice, serving until 1959
- After serving thee terms in the House, Marcos was elected to the Senate in 1959 and became the minority flood leader in 1960
- 1963 - 1965: he was Senate President
- During his 1962 campaign, he claimed to be the most decorated Filipino veteran of WWII, having garnered almost every medal and decoration awarded by the American or Philippine govt
- **The liberal party later confirmed that many of the medals were awarded to Marcos in 1962 to aid in his Senate election campaign**

# Presidency
## First Term (1965 - 1969)
- Maraming napagawa na infrastructure
- "Umayos" ang ekonomiya
- Dahil sa mga napagawa, na-boost ang kanyang imahe sa masa
### Dovie Beams Affair (1968 - 1970)
- Hollywood starlet to appear in the film "Maharlika"
- May relasyon kay Marcos
- Nag release ng cassette tape ng kanilang pagtalik
- Dahil dito sobrang gastor ng campaign niya para sa 2nd term dahil na rin pumangit ang imahe niya sa masa
- Dahil dito rin ang alleged election fraud

## Second Term (1969 - 1972)
- Alleged election fraud
- Marcos: "Two terms is enough for a man"
- Nagkaron ng usap-usapan sa mga plano ni Marcos para mapanatili sa kapangyarihan
	- Plan A: Gawing Parliamentary ang govt system para maging Prime Minister siya
	- Ideklara ang [[Proclamation No. 1081|Batas Militar]]
### Anti-government protests in early 1970
1. Inflation
2. [[Manuel Roxas#Military Bases Agreement (1946)|US Military Bases]]
3. Foreign control in the economy
4. Corruption
5. Widening gap between the rich and the poor
> Instead of reform, violences was used by the govt

#### First Quarter Storm
- Occured during the first three months of 1970s
- Sinugod ng mga activists ang malacanang

## Plaza Miranda Bombing
![[Pasted image 20260524153501.png|300]]
- August 21, 1971
- Plaza Miranda, Quiapo, downtown Manila
- Dito madalas na nagtitipon ang mga Activists and Politicians
- Campaign for Senatorial election
- Naghagis ng dalawang granada sa stage habang rally
- Hindi pa nakakarating si [[Ninoy Aquino|Aquino]] sa plaza kaya pinagbintangan siyang Komunista

## 1971 Constitutional Convention
- Initiated amid opposition calls for political and legal reforms, especially:
	- reform of elections
	- enforcement or challenge of the two-term presidential limit
	- Mas natugunan ng pansin kesa sa problema sa economic and social factors
	- Republic to Parliamentary
	- Binunyag ni [[Ninoy Aquino|Aquino]] na may doukmento na tinatawag na [[Proclamation No. 1081#Oplan Sagittarius|Oplan Sagittarius]], kung saan andong ang comprehensive plan para sa [[Proclamation No. 1081|Batas Militar]]

### Early push for Con-Con (1967)
- Opposition lawmakers in Congress passed [Resolution No. 2)](https://www.bing.com/search?q=resolution+no.2&cvid=5435a2f5962a405f8b6b537e7c668e5a&gs_lcrp=EgRlZGdlKgYIABBFGDkyBggAEEUYOTIGCAEQABhAMgYIAhAAGEAyBggDEAAYQDIGCAQQABhAMgYIBRAAGEAyBggGEAAYQDIGCAcQABhAMgYICBAAGEDSAQgyODA1ajBqOagCCLACAQ&FORM=ANAB01&PC=U531&source=chrome.ob)
- Called for a con-con to address reform issues
- [[Ferdinand E. Marcos|Marcos]] suprisingly endorsed it
- Historians note [[Ferdinand E. Marcos|Marcos]] likely supported it **hoping to remove or weaken the two-term limit**
### Controversy and Decline (1972)
- A major scandal emerged
- Implicated First Lady Imelda Marcos
- Allegedly, delagates were paid to support Marcos-aligned positions
### Termination of the convention
- In September 1972, Marcos declared [[Proclamation No. 1081|Martial Law]]
- The convention was effectively shut down
- 11 opposition delegates were arrested
- Constitutional reform process was halted and placed under authoritarian control
