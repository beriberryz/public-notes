	- Mas nabigyang tulong ang exports sa agrikultura

# Tugon ni Macapagal
- Stonehill Scandal (1962)
	- bribery scandal named after American expatriate businessman Harry Stonehill
	- implicated high level govt officials: [[Diosdado Macapagal|Macapagal]], [[Ferdinand E. Marcos|Marcos]], [[Carlos Garcia|Garcia]], and numerous other top Philippine officials who were accused of accepting bribes to protect Stonehill's $50-million business empire, which included a monopoly on tobacco and other exploited crops and popular local resources
- Hindi tinuloy ang mga agenda niya
- Tinanggal si Jose Diokno bilang justic secretary
	- Nag-imbestiga sa mga ilegal na transaksyon sa pagitan ng mga pulitiko at sa Amerikanong negosyante na si Harry Stonehill
