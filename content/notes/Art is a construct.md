# Art is a construct
- gawa-gawa lang
- can be true for the moment, but can’t be true forever (“someday we might not need art anymore”)

# The “autonomy” of art
## Magic
- transforming the natural to cultural
- Imagination → power over nature
	- Making sense of the world via myths and legends
	- Collective rituals make powerful magic
	- Epic poets were “possessed”
- Art = ritual

## Religion
- In early civilizations, religion, art, and ritual go together
- It would remain this way as societies develop
- In courtly society, art would become representation of the glory of monarchs → artists were sought for portraits

# Louis Althusser
- He considered art as ideological apparatus
- Ideology = “represent the imaginary relationship relationship of individuals to the conditions of existence”
- Ideological state apparatuses (ISA) → (media, school. family, religion, etc = institutions)
- Art is utilized by these institutions
